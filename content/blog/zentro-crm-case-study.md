# How I Built Zentro CRM from Idea to Interface

Building Zentro CRM was an exciting challenge that allowed me to blend my skills in product design with modern front-end development. 

## The Problem
Many CRM tools are either too complex for small teams or lack the necessary features to truly manage customer relationships effectively. I wanted to build something that was powerful, yet intuitive and clean.

## The Design Phase
I started with wireframes and user flows, focusing on:
- A clean, distraction-free dashboard
- Easy access to customer data
- A kanban-style project management board

## Front-end Implementation
Using Next.js and TypeScript, I brought the designs to life. I heavily focused on component reusability and maintaining a strict design system with CSS modules to ensure consistency across the application.
